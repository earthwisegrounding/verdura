FAQ

Are consultations really free?
Yes. Call us or send the quote form and we'll set up a free consultation to talk through your property and what you'd like to do with it.
Do you handle emergencies?
We do — we offer 24/7 emergency services, because landscape problems don't wait for business hours. Call 719-250-0747 anytime.
How does the 3D Design Studio work?
Open the studio, sketch your yard with trees, shrubs, walls, walkways, and more, then tap “Send my design for a free quote.” Your design file downloads and an email to our team opens — attach the file, send it, and we'll turn it into a real quote.
Do you work on commercial properties?
Yes. We provide commercial grounds maintenance and commercial snow removal, in addition to our residential services.
What forms of payment do you accept?
Cash, check, and all major credit cards — Visa, Mastercard, Discover, and American Express. A 4% service fee is added to credit card payments.
What are your hours?
Our office is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and closed weekends. Emergency service is available 24/7.
