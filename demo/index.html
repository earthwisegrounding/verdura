What we build

Everything on this plan is work we do. The numbers mark where each piece sits on the property above.

  1. Beaches and seawalls

    Sand and gravel beaches that slope gently into the water, or concrete bulkheads where the bank needs holding.

  2. Terraces and retaining walls

    Segmental block, poured concrete or timber, with drainage behind the wall so it stays put.

  3. Kitchen gardens

    Raised beds at a comfortable working height, with soil, irrigation and paths between them.

  4. Paths and lighting

    Walkways down to the water, lit low so they're safe at night without washing out the view.

  5. Outdoor rooms

    Patios, pergolas and fire pits placed for the evening sun and the view across the water.

  6. Planting and drainage

    Salt- and wind-tolerant planting, berms for screening, and dry creek beds that carry runoff to the shore.

How a project runs

  1. Sketch it in the studio

    Rough out ideas in 3D and send us the link, or skip this and we'll start from scratch on site.

  2. Walk the property with us

    We measure grades, soil and the high-water line, and check what shoreline rules allow.

  3. Get a drawn plan and a fixed price

    You'll see the design in 3D again, now to scale, with an itemized price we hold to.

  4. We build it

    One crew from first dig to final planting, and a walkthrough together on the last day.
